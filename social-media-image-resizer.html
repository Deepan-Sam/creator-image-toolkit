How to resize an image for social media

  1. Choose an image and a starting sizeUpload a JPG, PNG, or WebP, then select a platform and image use. Preset dimensions are editable.
  2. Choose the fitFit keeps the full image, Fill and crop trims the edges and supports adjustable positioning, and Stretch changes the proportions.
  3. Review and downloadCheck the preview and dimensions, choose an output format, create a verified image, and download it.

Platform image recommendations can change and vary by placement, device, and account. These sizes are practical editable starting points, not a guarantee of platform acceptance. Review current requirements before publishing.

Social media image resizing questions

Are these official or guaranteed platform dimensions?

No. Presets are practical, editable starting points. Requirements can vary by placement and change over time, so check the platform’s current guidance before publishing.

Is my selected image uploaded?

The resizer reads and renders the selected image in your browser and does not send it to an image-processing server. Hosting, advertising, and other website services may still make their own network requests; see the Privacy Policy.

Which image formats can I download?

JPEG, PNG, and WebP are offered when supported by your browser. JPEG does not preserve transparency; PNG and WebP can preserve transparent areas.
