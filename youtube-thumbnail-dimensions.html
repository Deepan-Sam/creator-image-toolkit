YouTube Thumbnail Dimensions and How to Resize an Image

For a standard horizontal YouTube video, 16:9 is the usual thumbnail shape. YouTube's current Help guidance lists 3840 × 2160 pixels for video thumbnails, with a minimum width of 640 pixels. A 1280 × 720 image is a common 16:9 working canvas; verify the latest official guidance before uploading.

Current thumbnail guidance

YouTube's official custom thumbnail guidance recommends high-resolution images, lists 3840 × 2160 for video thumbnails, and suggests 16:9 for standard videos. It lists JPG or PNG among supported formats. Upload-size limits vary by device: the current page states 2 MB for video thumbnails on mobile and 50 MB on desktop. Requirements can change, so verify the official page before publishing.

1280 × 720 is a convenient 16:9 working canvas, but it is not the only accepted resolution. YouTube currently recommends 9:16 for Shorts thumbnails, which use a different vertical presentation.

How to resize a thumbnail

  1. Choose a clear sourceA higher-resolution image gives more flexibility when cropping or reducing dimensions.
  2. Choose fit or cropFit keeps the entire picture visible but may leave space. Crop fills the frame but trims edges. Do not stretch a landscape image into a portrait frame.
  3. Position the subjectKeep faces, text and important objects inside the frame. Preview at a small size to check readability.
  4. Review format and file sizeUse a format accepted by YouTube and compare output size with the current limit for your upload device. Confirm official requirements before publishing.

Common problems

  • Subject looks stretched: preserve the ratio or crop.
  • Text is hard to read: use fewer words and preview at small size.
  • Details are cut off: reposition the crop or use fit with space.
  • Upload rejected: check official guidance for current formats, limits and eligibility.

YouTube thumbnail FAQ

Is 1280 × 720 the only recommended size?

No. Current YouTube Help lists 3840 × 2160 for video thumbnails and suggests 16:9. 1280 × 720 remains a common working canvas; check official guidance for updates.

Should a Shorts thumbnail be 16:9?

YouTube currently recommends 9:16 for Shorts thumbnails. Display behaviour can differ across YouTube surfaces.
