Tool guide

Prepare a 1280 × 720 thumbnail image

The thumbnail tool creates a 16:9, 1280 × 720 output from a JPG, PNG, or WebP image. Use fit to show the entire picture or crop to fill the frame, then adjust the crop position if needed.

Open the thumbnail resizer

This independent image tool is not affiliated with YouTube. Platform requirements can change.

How to make a 1280 × 720 thumbnail

  1. Choose an imageSelect a JPG, PNG, or WebP file, or drop one into the thumbnail tool. Its default file-size limit is 20 MB and can be changed in the editor.
  2. Choose fit or cropFit shows the full source image and adds space as needed. Crop fills the 16:9 frame and trims the edges.
  3. Adjust and previewWhen crop is selected, use the horizontal and vertical controls to position the visible area. Select a supported output format and review the preview.
  4. Download the verified outputThe tool enables download after verifying the generated 1280 × 720 image.

Thumbnail questions

What is the difference between fit and crop?

Fit preserves the whole image and leaves unused space around it. Crop fills the target frame but cuts off some of the source image.

Can I move the crop area?

Yes. In crop mode, adjust the horizontal and vertical position sliders to choose which part of the image remains visible.

Does 1280 × 720 guarantee that an image meets current YouTube requirements?

No. Platform requirements may change. Check the official YouTube Help guidance for video thumbnails before publishing.
