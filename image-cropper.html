How to crop an image

  1. Choose an imageSelect or drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP image. It is checked and decoded in your browser.
  2. Set the cropChoose freeform or a ratio preset, then move or resize the selection. You can also edit its pixel coordinates.
  3. Review and downloadChoose a supported output format, inspect the verified preview, and download a new file. Your original remains unchanged.
